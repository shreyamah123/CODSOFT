Who I Am

About Me

Hi! I'm Shreya Maheshwari, a second-year B.Tech Computer Science student at Shri Ram Murti Smarak College of Engineering and Technology, Bareilly. I completed my Class 12th from the CBSE board in 2024 with 76%, and finished my first year of engineering with a CGPA of 7.9.

I enjoy building things that solve real problems. In my very first year, I participated in the Smart India Hackathon and built an alcohol detection auto-cut circuit — a hardware device that prevents drunk driving by cutting the vehicle's ignition when alcohol is detected. I also developed a plant disease detection software as a field project, which helps farmers identify crop diseases early through image analysis.

I'm someone who values clear thinking, consistent learning, and doing good work. Every project I take on, I give it my full focus and always try to walk away having learned something new.
